    How it works

    1. Nemotron Nano turns what you wrote into a profile and asks what is missing.
    2. Nemotron Super plans the research; Tavily searches official sites and crawls the national scheme portal.
    3. Nano triages dozens of pages to the schemes that could fit you.
    4. Tavily extracts each scheme page; Super writes down every rule with a verbatim quote.
    5. Nemotron Ultra decides, rule by rule, whether you meet it, and what to ask if it cannot tell.
    6. Save the result and BenefitScout re-checks it for new schemes and changed deadlines.

    Nothing here is legal advice. Every verdict links to the page it was read from, so you can check it yourself.
